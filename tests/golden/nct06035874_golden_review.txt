INC-001 | INCLUSION
Source: A man or woman, 20 years of age or above with the diagnosis of type 2
diabetes for at least 3 months who meets all the following two criteria:
1. On standard anti-diabetic agents (metformin, DPP-4 inhibitors, sulphonylureas
or insulin, in any combination) with an HbA1c of \<9% at screening
2. Have documented hepatic steatosis (MRI-PDFF \>5.6%) on screening MRI-PDFF
